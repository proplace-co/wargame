Company Deep Dive

Mind map — what SOSUITES does

SOSUITES is a B2C platform that transforms a photo into a 5-second collaborative video series, where the community writes the continuation. It allows content creators and aspiring showrunners to co-write short, serialized stories, with a unique revenue-sharing promise where authors and producers receive one-third of the net profits per episode.

The solution uses AI to generate 5-second video clips with voice-overs and subtitles, and stands out with its collaborative continuation mechanism and per-episode remuneration system via Stripe. The business model is freemium with subscriptions (Creator, Studio, Pro) and episode purchases at €5.99, which trigger revenue sharing. The promotional kit (posters, trailers) aims to empower creator-led distribution.

The company initially targets French-speaking social users and micro-creators, leveraging ease of mobile production and financial incentives against established platforms like TikTok and Instagram. Although B2B potential exists via the Pro plan for brands, SOSUITES is primarily positioned as a social entertainment and collaborative content creation platform.
