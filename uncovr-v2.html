Company Deep Dive

Mind map — what Uncovr does

Uncovr is developing an AI infrastructure that transforms live surgical videos into structured, actionable clinical intelligence. The platform analyzes operation and endoscopy videos in real time, automatically drafts operative reports, and suggests procedure and billing (CPT) codes even before the surgeon leaves the operating room. This solution aims to bridge the gap between what actually happens during the procedure and what is documented, thereby eliminating missed billable steps and reimbursement discrepancies.

The company targets hospital systems, particularly those with high-volume robotic and minimally invasive surgery programs, revenue cycle management teams, and surgeons eager to reduce administrative burden. Its business model is based on a B2B SaaS subscription, with a zero-PHI architecture and on-device anonymization to ensure patient data privacy. Uncovr positions itself as the system of record for surgery, aspiring to become the Epic of the operating room.
