Company Deep Dive

Mind map — what Plurai does

Plurai is a reliability platform for AI agents, enabling AI engineering teams and developers to deploy reliable, safe, and cost-effective agents in production. It uses simulation to detect failures and post-training to correct them, thereby optimizing agent intelligence and cost.

The solution creates a digital twin of the agent and its environment from the company's knowledge base, tools, and policies. It simulates thousands of realistic and adversarial users in a sandbox environment to expose failures before deployment. The simulation data is then used to post-train a smaller, task-specific model, making it more accurate and significantly cheaper to run. Plurai unifies simulation, evaluation, guardrails, and optimization in a single lifecycle.

The business model is B2B SaaS with tiered subscriptions and usage-based pricing, including a free plan, token-based plans for optimized SLM and LLM models, and custom enterprise pricing. The company has raised $13 million in seed capital and is used by developers at major companies like Microsoft, Google, and NVIDIA, demonstrating significant reductions in inference costs, latency, and production failures.
