Company Deep Dive

Mind map — what Maestrohub does

MaestroHub is an industrial context platform that unifies fragmented machine and enterprise system data in factories. It transforms this data into a single, governed language, accessible to people and AI agents, to improve operational visibility and optimize performance.

The solution is natively based on a Unified Namespace (UNS) and offers over 140 connectors to integrate OT/IT data (PLC, SCADA, MES, ERP). It enables use cases such as predictive maintenance, reduction of energy waste, and the creation of AI-driven work orders. MaestroHub operates on a SaaS model with an annual per-site subscription, targeting multi-site manufacturers in sectors like automotive, food & beverage, and pharmaceuticals.

Founded in 2023, the Turkish company (with a German subsidiary) has raised $1.2M and counts names like Ford Otosan and BSH among its clients. Its CEO, Yasir Tunçer, brings deep expertise in industrial engineering and automation, having spent over seven years at Ford Otosan.
