Market DEFINITION

The Swiss market for travel insurance and international assistance, covering private and professional travel of the resident population and employees on mission in Switzerland and abroad. ➜ The buyer is a Swiss resident, a corporate travel manager or an HR and compliance officer purchasing annual medical, cancellation and assistance cover for a specific trip or a specific population of travellers, and the job being hired is to make an emergency abroad financially and administratively survivable.

The structural friction is that the Swiss LAMal regime only reimburses emergency medical costs outside the EU and EFTA up to twice the Swiss cost of the same treatment, so the traveller is exposed to a gap that a consumer-grade annual policy of CHF 85 to CHF 300 does not visibly close, while the corporate buyer has an ISO 31030 documentation obligation that no incumbent consumer product was built to satisfy.[?] Upstream sit the risk carriers and reinsurers who underwrite the policies, downstream sit the brokers, mobility consultants and booking platforms who own the customer relationship, and the profit pool concentrates with the carriers and the three dominant distributors — ERV at 58%, Allianz Partners at 25% and Hanse Merkur at 11% — which means a new entrant must either capture the distribution margin or move toward risk-bearing to escape the squeeze.[?]

Our Market THESIS

The Swiss travel insurance market has crossed a threshold that cannot be undone: the ISO 31030 duty-of-care standard has turned corporate travel risk from a discretionary perk into a documented obligation, and the LAMal reimbursement cap abroad has made private international cover a compliance line item rather than a nice-to-have, in a market measured at USD 0.19 Billion in 2025 and forecast at USD 0.46 Billion by 2034.[?] The competitive data does not allow us to claim that ERV, Allianz Partners or Hanse Merkur are structurally unable to respond — they hold 58%, 25% and 11% of the Swiss market respectively and have the capital and carrier relationships to build a corporate product if they choose to — so the honest reading is that their consumer books give them no urgent reason to prioritise a niche that represents a small fraction of their revenue, not that they cannot.[?] The attack vector is the white-label distribution layer: Maximum does not need to win the traveller, it needs to be the checkbox inside the booking flow of the brokers, mobility consultants and private aviation operators who already own the executive relationship, which is a distribution position the incumbents have no incentive to contest because it cannibalises their direct consumer channel.

The window is open now because the ISO 31030 obligation is recent and corporate buyers are still choosing their documentation vendor, and it closes when one of the three incumbents launches a corporate duty-of-care product or when a well-funded InsurTech buys the broker network — realistically a two to three year window.[?]

Mind map — what Maximum Insurance does

Maximum Insurance is a Swiss international travel insurance broker, registered with FINMA, offering a hybrid insurance and software solution. It targets executives, companies with more than 5 employees, and HNWIs, providing 24/7 protection, access to a global medical network, and automated claim preparation for flight delays or cancellations.

Its offering includes a "Travel Account" and a "Duty of Care Cockpit" which documents ISO 31030 compliance for corporate travel risk management. The business model is based on annual B2B and B2C subscriptions, with packages such as the VIP Pack (USD 990/year/employee) and the VIP Extreme Pack (USD 2290/year/employee), as well as a white-label partnership program and a data-sharing program for closed claims files.

The company positions itself as a key player for corporate mobility and executive protection, integrating its solution into the booking flows of brokers, mobility consultants, and private aviation operators. The team, led by Charles-Edouard Busson (CEO), is a small Swiss team focused on compliance and premium service, with no public fundraising or numerous verified customer testimonials to date.
